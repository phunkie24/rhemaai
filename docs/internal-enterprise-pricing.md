Cost assumptions behind these margins
State these explicitly so the floors can be re-derived if salaries or FX move — don't just trust the historical numbers.
•	Assumed fully-loaded engineer cost: ₦2.5M/month (senior), ₦1.5M–₦2.5M/month (mid-level)
•	Target gross margin on managed-service contracts: 45–60%
•	Assessment/advisory engagements assumed to consume ≤1 person-week of senior time
•	PoC engagements assumed to consume 2–4 person-weeks
•	Production deployments assumed to consume 1–3 person-months, 1–2 people
•	24/7 coverage requires rotational staffing (minimum 3 people) — priced accordingly, never quoted against a single hire

## Website implementation notes — 11 September 2026

- The detailed v2 product and service rows take precedence over general Nigerian floors where they differ.
- All product PoCs use the explicitly supplied international price of $20,000, independently of the Nigerian price.
- The eight explicit Nexus AOS price pairs are published exactly as supplied.
- Other existing product USD prices are retained subject to the v2 international floors: assessment $3,500, production and enterprise deployment $50,000, and professional managed operations $15,000 monthly. Axiom QR enterprise therefore also meets the $50,000 production floor. No USD values are calculated from NGN.
- Revised service stages publish their supplied Nigerian prices. The previously approved international enterprise engagement is kept as a separate USD-only row because v2 supplies no replacement service-specific USD list.
- General managed-service packages use the explicit international floors of $15,000 (Professional), $30,000 (Enterprise), and $40,000 (24/7 operations) monthly. Essential has no supplied USD rate and shows its NGN price only. Nexus Mission-Critical AgentOps uses its specific $50,000 rate.
- Prior Nigerian prices and upper ranges are archived under `archive/internal-enterprise-pricing-v1.md` for historical reference, not current quotes.
- Delivery costs, salaries, staffing assumptions and margins are internal and are not bundled into the website.
