## Important commercial rule

Use separate Nigerian and international price lists. Do not mechanically convert the Nigerian price into dollars: international pricing reflects larger budgets, higher liability, insurance expectations, procurement effort, documentation, compliance and support requirements.

On every page, add:

> **Enterprise engagements start from the displayed amount. Final pricing depends on data volume, integrations, deployment environment, security requirements, number of workflows, service levels and delivery timeline. Cloud usage, model/API consumption, licences, hardware, travel and taxes are charged separately.**
